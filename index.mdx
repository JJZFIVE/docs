EasyPPP adjusts your Stripe prices to local purchasing power. Setup is three steps and takes about five minutes.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Getting started">
    Open [app.easyppp.com](https://app.easyppp.com/sign-up) and create your account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect Stripe">
    Approve access on Stripe and you are returned to EasyPPP. We read your products, prices and past payments. We never charge, refund or move money.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make prices">
    Pick the products to localize and choose your markets. EasyPPP creates a real Stripe Price for each market on your existing products.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What happens next

Your localized prices live on your own Stripe Products, visible in the Stripe Dashboard and the API. Your original price stays as the base and the fallback, so buyers outside your chosen markets pay exactly what they pay today.
